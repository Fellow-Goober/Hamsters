What are Hamsters?

Hamsters are rat-like beings (rodents) that usually have a small tail. They are playful, sociable, and usually range from 2 - 7 inches in length. They usually live for one and a half to years. Hamsters come in many colors, but the most common are tan, white, black, and many others. They can even be kept as pets!!!
